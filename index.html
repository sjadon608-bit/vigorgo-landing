Why we built it different

We studied every EV rental fleet on the road — then chose not to build another one of them.

Before developing this project, our team studied multiple EV rental models across the market. We found most fleets leaning on imported, plastic-bodied scooters — cheap to deploy, but contributing to rising plastic waste and long-term environmental strain.

So VigorGo takes a different path. Every vehicle is engineered with approximately 85% metal construction — stronger, more durable, easier to repair, and far more sustainable across its lifecycle. This isn't just a product launch. It's the start of a cleaner, smarter way to move India's cities.
